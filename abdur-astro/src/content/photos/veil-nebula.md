The Veil Nebula is what remains of a star about twenty times the Sun's mass
that tore itself apart some ten to twenty thousand years ago. Located 2,400
light-years away in Cygnus, the shockwave has opened into a loop three
degrees across: six times the width of the full Moon. The red color comes
from ionized hydrogen gas, and the blue comes from oxygen. The death of a
star such as this one seeds the universe with the heavier elements needed
for life.
