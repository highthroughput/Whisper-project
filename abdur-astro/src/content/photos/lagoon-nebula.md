From Central Alberta the Lagoon never climbs higher than about thirteen degrees — it
skims the southern treeline for a couple of hours and sinks again. Every sub in this
stack was shot through five times more atmosphere than an overhead target, which is
the price of chasing Sagittarius from 52° north.

It's worth chasing. M 8 is the Milky Way's showpiece star factory, close enough that
the dark lagoon channel splitting the cloud is obvious even in short exposures. Shot
over six July nights from a ridge with a clean southern horizon; narrowband cut
through the low-altitude murk that would have washed out broadband colour.
