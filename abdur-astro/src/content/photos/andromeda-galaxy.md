A collection of about one trillion stars like our Sun — and so far away that the
light in this picture left the galaxy 2.6 million years ago. Andromeda will collide
with our own Milky Way in about 5 billion years, but until then, this is our closest
large galactic neighbour. Across the full frame, regions of intense star formation,
intricate dust lanes, and even individual resolved stars are visible within the
galaxy's disk.

Shot from a backyard in Cochrane, Alberta, over more than three months — accumulating
data between August 15 and November 20, 2023.
