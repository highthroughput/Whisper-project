The quiet drama of this one is scale: a perfect sphere seven light-years across,
inflated by the wind of a single star you can point to in the frame. As a print it
works like a held breath — one luminous form centred in folds of darker nebulosity,
calm from a distance, violent in the caption.

A strong choice for offices and studies; the composition stays legible at 12 × 18″
and becomes immersive at 24 × 36″. Matte suits it especially well, keeping the
highlights soft against the deep field behind them.
