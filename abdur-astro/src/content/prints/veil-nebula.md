The most painterly object in the northern sky. The Veil's braided red and teal
filaments read almost like brushwork until you get close and the star field snaps into
focus — thousands of points, each one sharp. It's the print people assume is an
artwork first and a photograph second, and it starts conversations accordingly.

The interleaved emission colours are the print's structure, so colour fidelity
matters: proofed on both papers, with the teal oxygen fronts holding their
separation from the hydrogen red even in warm evening room light.
