A vertical subject that wants a vertical wall — stairwells and entryways love this
print. The pillars rise through the frame the way they rise through the Eagle Nebula,
edges glowing where starlight is boiling them away. The Hubble-palette golds and teals
sit comfortably against almost any interior.

This is the portfolio's limited piece: an edition of fifty, each print signed and
numbered in the margin, with the acquisition data — 29.5 hours across nine short
summer nights — inscribed on the certificate that ships with it.
