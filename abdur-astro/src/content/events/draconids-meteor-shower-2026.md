---
title: 'Draconids meteor shower'
kind: 'astronomical'
date: 2026-10-08
when: 'Peak night, Oct 8'
location: 'Best after dark, low bright-sky interference'
---

Unlike most showers, the Draconids favour the evening over the pre-dawn hours,
radiating from the head of Draco high in the northern sky as soon as it gets
dark. Rates are usually modest, but the shower has produced unpredictable
outbursts in the past.
