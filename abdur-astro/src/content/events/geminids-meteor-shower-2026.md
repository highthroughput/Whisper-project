---
title: 'Geminids meteor shower'
kind: 'astronomical'
date: 2026-12-13
when: 'Peak night, Dec 13'
location: 'Visible most of the night once Gemini clears the horizon'
---

The most reliable major shower of the year, often producing well over a
meteor a minute at its peak under a dark sky. Bundle up: this one rewards
patience more than any other night on the calendar.
