---
title: 'Orionids meteor shower'
kind: 'astronomical'
date: 2026-10-21
when: 'Peak night, Oct 21'
location: 'Best after midnight, once Orion has climbed higher'
---

Debris from Halley's Comet, burning up as fast, often bright meteors that
radiate from near Orion's club. A dependable mid-autumn shower for anyone
willing to stay out past midnight.
