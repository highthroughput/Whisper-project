---
title: 'Leonids meteor shower'
kind: 'astronomical'
date: 2026-11-17
when: 'Peak night, Nov 17'
location: 'Best after midnight, radiant rises with Leo'
---

Fast, often bright meteors from the dust trail of Comet Tempel-Tuttle. Rates
are moderate most years, but the Leonids are the shower known for producing
storm-level activity in rare cycles.
